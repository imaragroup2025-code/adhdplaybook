What this addresses
If these four things are running your life, keep reading
Based on what real ADHD communities talk about most — these are not edge cases. They are the everyday reality.
🧱

Executive dysfunction

Knowing exactly what to do and not being able to make yourself do it. The guide explains why this happens neurologically and gives you ignition tools that actually work with your brain.

⏳

Time paralysis

Time blindness is real. "Later" and "now" feel the same distance away. Learn how to make time visible and tangible so it stops disappearing on you.

💭

Overthinking

Two hundred browser tabs running in your head, none of them closing. The guide gives you a capture system and a "parking lot" method that quiets the loop.

🔁

Task completion

The valley between starting and finishing is where ADHD brains get stuck most. Learn how to close loops without relying on willpower or motivation.
